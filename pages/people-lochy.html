Lochy

Lochy brings a mix of chaotic humour, solid building skills, and a strangely unshakeable confidence to every season. In Season 2 he stepped into a leadership role as the king of the Volcanic team, guiding a group that was equal parts brilliant and unhinged. He plays for fun first, but somehow still ends up in the middle of half the major storylines.

Overview

Lochy describes himself simply as “lochy,” which honestly tells you most of what you need to know. He joins each season for the fun of it, but in Season 2 he unexpectedly became the king of the Volcanic team — a role he handled with a mix of instinct, improvisation, and whatever energy he had that day.

He’s the kind of player who can go from joking around to accidentally shaping the entire season without even realising it.

Abilities

Lochy’s strongest skills are building and team organisation. He’s surprisingly good at pulling people together and keeping things moving, even if he pretends he has no idea what’s going on.

  • Builder: Creative, fast, and always down to help.
  • Team Organiser: Somehow gets people coordinated.
  • Chaos Navigator: Thrives in unpredictable situations.

When asked for a moment where he used these skills in a surprising way, he responded: “jackson why are we making questions hard now :(” which is, in its own way, a perfect answer.

Lore & Background

One of the moments that shaped Lochy the most was jumping into a LEGO mascot in the UK. No further explanation was provided, and honestly, none is needed.

He also claims he “used to be pretty sigma,” which may or may not be true, but he says it with enough confidence that you just kind of accept it.

Lochy’s past is a mix of questionable decisions, strange adventures, and stories that sound fake but probably aren’t.

  • Criminal Mastermind: One of his proudest arcs, and a moment where his mix of humour and unpredictability shaped the story in unexpected ways.
