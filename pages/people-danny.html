Danny

Danny builds things. Big things, weird things, things nobody asked for. He's also the one trying to calm everyone down when a season starts falling apart.

Overview

Danny doesn't really play to win. He plays to hang out, explore and make cool stuff with whoever's around.

He's also the server's unofficial peacekeeper. When two teams are about to go at it, Danny is usually standing in the middle trying to talk everyone out of it.

Abilities

His real superpower is diplomacy. He can keep a lid on things even when everyone is fed up with each other.

  • Natural diplomat: sorts out arguments before they turn into wars.
  • Creative brain: if there's a way to fix something without a fight, he'll find it.
  • Community builder: builds places people actually want to hang out in.

His biggest diplomatic win: turning the Museum of St Pluto into a neutral zone that every side respected, even in the middle of a war.

Lore & Background

Danny's backstory is simple: he's always liked making things out of nothing. He says this started at birth. He also says he was "born at a very young age", so make of that what you will.

He keeps the rest of his history vague on purpose. It's funnier that way.

Season Highlights

  • St Pluto's Museum: his proudest build. A peaceful landmark that ended up being one of the most important places of the season.
  • Biggest mistake: in his own words, "joining". He's (probably) joking.
