Two Titans, One Broken World

Ignivar (fire) and Verdantis (life) built the world together, right up until Ignivar went bad. Their fight split the land into two islands and left bits of space that don't work properly any more.

The Seasons

Season 1 was the first go, with everyone working it out as they went. Season 2 brought the wall, the Titans and a full-on war. Season 3 is being planned.

The Players

Seventeen of us so far. Everyone gets a page with their ability, their team and a 3D model of their skin you can spin around.

Abilities

Flight. Invincibility. Stealing other people's abilities. Summoning golems. A flame sword of doom. And a pig called Harold. Most of them you have to earn.

The Map

The Magma Forge, the Obsidian Spine, the Ashen Colosseum, the Whispering Waterfall, and a bridge that's 20 blocks long but takes 60 to cross. Fly around the whole thing in 3D.

Legendary Items

Ignivar's Gauntlet, the Bloodmoon Bow, the Wings of the Ironclad. The best gear in the game, and none of it is easy to get.
