Johan

Johan is the quiet one. He's usually off somewhere reading, tinkering with whatever the mods added this season, or digging a tunnel nobody knew about.

Overview

What gets Johan to log on is the mods. New mechanics, weird magic systems, things that break the normal rules of Minecraft. If something new got added, he's already poking at it.

He doesn't say much, but having him on your team makes things calmer. Someone has to be the sensible one.

Abilities

Magic. Enchanted gear, modded spells, anything that bends the rules a bit.

  • Mod brain: figures out new modded mechanics faster than anyone.
  • Low profile: uses his abilities so quietly you don't notice until it's done.
  • Book smart: has read a lot. It shows.

He's not one for big flashy moments, but a lot of what goes right for his team has his fingerprints on it.

Lore & Background

Johan's past is mostly books. That's where the calm, curious side of him comes from: he'd rather understand a problem than hit it with a sword.

He keeps the rest of his backstory to himself.

Season Highlights

  • The tunnel: his proudest build. A huge tunnel that became one of the landmarks of the season.
  • Low point: not being able to log on for a stretch and missing out on a chunk of the action.

Stats

  • Puns: ∞/10

Trivia

  • Rated his own stupidity 10/10 and his knowledge 9/10. Both are probably accurate.
