Season Overview

Each season of Popularis introduces new gameplay mechanics, new lore, and new worldbuilding elements. Season 1 established the foundation of the universe, while Season 2 expanded the world with Titans, riddles, and non‑Euclidean structures. More seasons are planned, with new islands, new mechanics, and new storylines.
