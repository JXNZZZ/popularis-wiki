Mark

Mark is good at this game and he knows it. Fast, smart, scary in a fight, and always trying to do something nobody's tried before. When Mark is involved, things get intense.

Overview

Mark likes to say he's the most powerful person to exist. That's a joke. Mostly. What actually drives him is doing something unique each season: a plan, a build or a play that nobody else would think of.

He can work with a team or go full one-man army, and he thinks everything through before he does it. That combination is why people don't like fighting him.

Abilities

Brains, combat, and getting things done properly. Give him a goal, however ridiculous, and he'll find a way.

  • Combat: fast, precise and very hard to beat.
  • Strategy: works out the best move while everyone else is still panicking.
  • Adapts quickly: figures out how you fight, then counters it.
  • Jack of all trades: good at things that aren't even his thing.

Asked for a time he used these skills in a surprising way, he said: "The entire season." Fair enough.

Lore and Background

Mark thinks logically about everything: how he plays, how he plans, how he deals with people. He admits he wasn't always like this and used to be "a lot less intellectual". Character development.

Season Highlights

  • Season 1: led his team to victory. "Technically," he says.
  • Season 2: his biggest mistake was making it a bit too obvious he wasn't fully on his team's side. They noticed.

Stats

  • Speed: 8/10
  • Combat: 9/10
  • Intelligence: 1/10 (self-rated, "merit master")

Trivia

  • Rated his own intelligence 1/10 straight after telling us he's the most powerful person alive.
