How Abilities Work

You get your ability by doing its quest, finding its location, or picking its origin. Some are simple and some are ridiculous, but they all fit the island they belong to: fire stuff on the volcano, nature stuff in the jungle.

Legendary Abilities

A few abilities come from legendary items or Titan-forged relics. You have to solve riddles, go somewhere dangerous or beat a boss to get them. They're some of the strongest things in the game, and every one has a story attached.
