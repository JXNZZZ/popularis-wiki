Robert

Robert describes himself as maybe possibly probably most likely indubitably undoubtedly Robert the cool sigma epic fella. He has not played a season yet, but his presence alone suggests he will arrive with infinite aura and a level of confidence that cannot be taught.

Overview

Robert enters each season motivated by one thing. The fact that he has not actually entered a season yet. His potential is limitless and completely untested, which somehow makes him even more dangerous.

He claims his strongest ability is his small hitbox, which is impressive considering he has never used it. His entire identity is built on the idea that when he finally appears, everyone else should be worried.

Abilities

Robert’s abilities are theoretical but powerful. He has not played, but the legend of his skills grows anyway.

  • Small Hitbox: Hard to hit. Hard to see. Hard to comprehend.
  • Incredible Height: Somehow both tall and small at the same time.
  • Pretty Smart: Claims high intelligence with no evidence required.
  • Infinite Aura: Radiates energy that cannot be measured.

When asked for a moment where he used his abilities in a surprising way, he simply said he did not because he has not played. This only adds to the mystery.

Lore and Background

The moment that shaped Robert the most was his birth. A lesser known detail about his past is that he was born. These facts are simple but powerful, and they define him more than any season ever could.

Robert exists in a strange space between myth and reality. He has not played, yet everyone knows who he is. He has no achievements, yet he speaks like a veteran. His lore is built on potential, and that potential is terrifying.
