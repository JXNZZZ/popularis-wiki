About the Players

Popularis is really just its players. Some of them plan everything. Some of them cause chaos. Some are villains by accident, and a couple are villains on purpose.

Everyone gets a page with what they're good at, their backstory, their best (and worst) moments, and a 3D model of their skin you can spin around.

How This Works

  • Select any player from the bottom navigation.
  • Each player page includes their overview, abilities, lore, highlights, stats, and trivia.
  • The sidebar on each page helps you jump between sections easily.
  • The 3D skin viewer shows their in game appearance.

Where to start

Not sure? Greer's page has a cursed pig in it. Robert hasn't played a single season and is somehow still the most confident person here. Start with one of them.
