Where it all started.

The first season. Everyone turned up on the islands, nobody knew what was going on, and a lot of what later seasons are built on started here.

Overview

The Premise

You spawn on a weird archipelago with zero instructions. Season 1 was mostly about exploring, staying alive and finding the first bits of lore lying around.

The Cast

The Teams

Teams were small and pretty loose. Alliances changed all the time, and most of the drama came from not having enough stuff and the world trying to kill you.

Powers

Abilities

Abilities were simple back then: small boosts from relics and things you found. The bigger ability system in later seasons grew out of this.

Timeline

Major Events

The big moments were mostly firsts: first dungeon cleared, first relic found, first hint that something bigger was going on with the islands.

Difficulty

The Early Challenges

There were no phases or walls yet. The challenge was just the world: rough terrain, not much to go around, and ruins you probably shouldn't have walked into.

Backstory

The Lore

Old ruins and strange carvings hinted at a civilisation that had disappeared. Nobody knew it yet, but this was the first sign of the Titans and the story that Season 2 picks up.
