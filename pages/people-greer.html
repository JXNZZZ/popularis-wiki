Meet the players of Popularis: explorers, fighters, strategists, builders, and quiet villains who shape the story through their choices and the chaos they leave behind.

Greer (Zoblii)

Greer looks harmless at first glance, but anyone who played Season 2 knows the truth. She is quiet, unpredictable, and backed by Harold, a creature so unsettling that even the owner of the server, Jackson, somehow died to him while in creative mode. Greer explores, builds, hides, and smiles through it all, but beneath that calm exterior is one of the most dangerous players the Jungle ever produced.

Overview

Greer’s username is Zoblii, which somehow fits her perfectly. She enters each season with one main motivation: Harold. Everything she does revolves around him. Every plan, every structure, every quiet moment is just her waiting for Harold to do something terrifying.

She loves exploring, collecting soap, and building Harold shrines in places no one expects. In Season 2 Jungle, she quietly became one of the most powerful villains in the entire world, influencing events from the shadows while everyone else was busy fighting each other.

Abilities

Greer’s strongest skills are hiding, building, and weaponising Harold. She is patient, observant, and frighteningly calm in situations where everyone else panics.

  • Hiding: Can vanish from the world for long stretches, only to reappear at the worst possible moment.
  • Builder: Creates shrines, hideouts, and structures that feel unsettlingly intentional.
  • Harold Handler: Commands a creature capable of killing players who should not be killable.
  • Silent Villainy: Appears harmless until the moment she decides not to be.

When asked for a specific moment where she used these abilities in a surprising way, she said she did not really have one. The truth is that her entire presence is a slow burn threat that most people do not notice until it is too late.

Lore and Background

When asked what part of her past shaped her the most, Greer simply said "Hi." It fits her style. She later revealed a lesser known detail: she is secretly evil. She even delivered it with a dramatic pause.

In Season 1 on the Arctic team, she built a small Harold shrine in the underground house. In Season 2 Jungle, she escalated. Harold shrines appeared in the wild. Harold himself became a force of nature. And Greer, quietly watching from the trees, turned into one of the most powerful villains in the entire season.

Season Highlights

  • Harold’s Impossible Kill: Harold somehow killed Jackson while he was in creative mode. No one understands how. Greer was delighted.
  • Harold vs Her Own Team: Her proudest moment was Harold murdering her own teammates.
  • Season 2 Absence: Her biggest regret was not logging in during the major fights so Harold could finish the job.
